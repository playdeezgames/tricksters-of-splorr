Title: Tricksters of SPLORR!! is on the stage

Short version: a small stealth puzzle game, ten levels, no fighting, made for All Tools Allowed #3 (theme: TRICK). It runs in your browser.

You are a trickster. Every level is a stage with a prize on it, an exit across it, and guards who would like to see you. Guards watch the three tiles in front of them, and what they do is never random. You can see their route, watch their cone, and plan.

Four tricks, each taught by a level you cannot win without it: toss a coin to draw a guard away, lift a key from a guard who is not looking, point past a guard so he turns his head, and borrow a hat that fools guards of its own colour.

Every level has a par, the fewest turns that wins. A solver written for the tests checks that each level can be won and that the par on screen is the true minimum, so if you beat it, tell me, because I would like to see how. Undo and restart are free and unlimited.

Keyboard needed for now: arrow keys or WASD, Space to wait, T, L, P for the tricks, Z to undo.

There is a small surprise in the last act. Take a bow when it comes.

How it was made: Odin compiled to WebAssembly, no engine, written with Claude Code from my design decisions and playtesting. The full disclosure is on the page.

Still on the list: touch controls, and more stages.
